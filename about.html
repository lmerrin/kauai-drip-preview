Ashley’s story

Healthcare that looks at the whole picture.

Ashley Ilustre Lizama, MSN, APRN, FNP-C, is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with more than a decade of healthcare experience.

She began her career as a registered nurse in 2013 and developed experience in inpatient care, urgent care, healthcare leadership, acute care and preventive medicine. Her work evolved from bedside nursing into administration and leadership, including serving as Executive Director of a local urgent care while continuing to care for patients.

Kauaʻi Drip began in 2021 with a vision to bring more personalized, proactive wellness care to the community. Ashley had seen people struggle with fatigue, weight changes, hormonal symptoms, brain fog, low energy and changes related to aging—often without the time or support to understand the full picture.

What began with IV hydration evolved into a comprehensive health-optimization clinic combining clinical experience, continued education, comprehensive lab interpretation and individualized treatment planning.

The philosophy remains simple: listen closely, look deeper and treat each person as an individual.
