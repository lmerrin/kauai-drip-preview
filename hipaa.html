Patient privacy

Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, how you can access it and your rights regarding your health information.

Draft migration for Ashley’s review: This redesign preserves the substance of Kauaʻi Drip’s currently published notice. Kauaʻi Drip must confirm the effective date, applicability and final wording before replacing the existing approved notice.

Effective date: To be confirmed before launch.

Our commitment and duties

Kauaʻi Drip, LLC is a healthcare provider that complies with HIPAA and its implementing Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules as applicable to the practice. Kauaʻi Drip is required to maintain the privacy and security of protected health information (PHI), provide this notice, notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured PHI when required, and follow the notice currently in effect.

How health information may be used or disclosed

Treatment

PHI may be used or shared to provide, coordinate or manage care, including with physicians, hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, imaging facilities, specialists and other providers involved in treatment.

Payment and healthcare operations

PHI may be used for payment activities when applicable and for operations such as quality assessment, patient safety, training, credentialing, licensing, compliance, audits, legal services, business planning and customer service. Although the practice is primarily cash-pay, payment-related uses may still occur.

Business associates

Vendors and contractors may receive PHI when needed to perform services. When HIPAA requires it, written business-associate agreements require those parties to safeguard PHI.

Other permitted or required purposes

Information may be used or disclosed when permitted or required by law for public health and safety, reporting abuse or neglect, health oversight, legal proceedings, law enforcement, coroners or medical examiners, organ donation, approved research, serious threats to health or safety, specialized government functions and workers’ compensation.

Uses generally requiring authorization

Uses not described in the notice generally require written authorization, including most uses of psychotherapy notes, marketing uses requiring authorization and disclosures constituting a sale of PHI. Kauaʻi Drip states that it does not sell PHI. A written authorization may generally be revoked prospectively.

Your rights

  • Inspect or obtain an electronic or paper copy of qualifying records.
  • Ask to correct information believed to be incorrect or incomplete.
  • Request reasonable confidential communications.
  • Ask for restrictions on certain uses or disclosures, subject to applicable rules.
  • Request an accounting of certain disclosures.
  • Request a paper copy of the current notice.
  • Choose a legally authorized personal representative.
  • Receive breach notification when required by law.
  • File a complaint without retaliation.

Complaints

Contact Kauaʻi Drip’s Privacy Officer using the information below. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights through HHS’s complaint information or by calling 1-877-696-6775. Kauaʻi Drip will not retaliate for a complaint.

Changes to this notice

Kauaʻi Drip may change this notice and its privacy practices. A revised notice may apply to PHI already maintained. The current approved notice and effective date will be made available at the clinic and on the website.
