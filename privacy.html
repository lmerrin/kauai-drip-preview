Website Privacy Policy

How information associated with the public Kauaʻi Drip website may be handled.

Draft for Ashley’s review: This page is a working website-policy draft and must be reviewed and approved by Kauaʻi Drip before launch. It does not replace the separate HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Proposed effective date: To be confirmed before launch.

Scope

This policy applies to the public website operated for Kauaʻi Drip, LLC. Information created or maintained as part of medical care may be protected by HIPAA and is addressed in the separate HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information handled by this website

The website does not currently provide a first-party contact form, patient portal, payment form, account system, advertising tracker or analytics tool. If you call, text or email through a displayed link, your communication is handled by your service provider and Kauaʻi Drip’s communication systems.

Web hosts and security providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security events to deliver and protect the website.

Third-party services

  • Jane: appointment scheduling, required intake, gift cards and packages are provided through Kauaʻi Drip’s Jane booking site and are subject to Jane and Kauaʻi Drip’s applicable notices.
  • Google Maps: the embedded map and directions link may allow Google to receive technical and usage information under its own policies.
  • Google Fonts: the website may request font files from Google to display the approved typography.
  • Instagram: following the social link takes you to Instagram and its policies.

Medical and sensitive information

Do not use ordinary email, text messaging or the public website to submit urgent, highly sensitive or detailed medical information. Use Kauaʻi Drip’s approved secure process. For an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Sharing and selling

Kauaʻi Drip does not sell personal information or mobile opt-in data. Information may be handled by service providers supporting website delivery and clinic operations, when directed or authorized by you, or when permitted or required by law.

Security and retention

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained as required for the purpose collected and applicable legal, operational and recordkeeping obligations.

Children

This public website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a website form.

Changes and contact

This draft may be revised as the website, vendors or legal requirements change. Questions may be directed to Kauaʻi Drip, LLC at ashley@kauaidrip.com, (808) 470-7970, or 4-1579 Kūhiō Highway, Suites 205 and 207, Kapaʻa, HI 96746.
