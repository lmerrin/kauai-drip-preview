East Kauaʻi Professional Building

Easy to find. Simple to arrive.

4-1579 Kūhiō Highway, Suites 205 & 207, Kapaʻa, HI 96746

The clinic is on the second floor. Please check in at Suite 207.

  1. 1

    Drive up the north-end ramp

    Follow the ramp at the north end of the building to the second-floor parking area.

  2. 2

    Park in the upper lot

    Parking is free, and the upper parking area provides wheelchair-accessible arrival.

  3. 3

    Enter through the north entrance

    Kauaʻi Drip is the first corner suite on the left. Please check in at Suite 207.
