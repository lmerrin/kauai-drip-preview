Frequently asked questions

Helpful information before you book.

Care should feel personal, informed and never rushed. These general answers do not replace individualized medical advice.

General

What is Kauaʻi Drip?

Kauaʻi Drip is a personalized health-optimization clinic offering comprehensive lab testing, hormone and health optimization, medical weight management, IV therapy, wellness injections, peptide therapy, skin-health treatments, body contouring and longevity-focused care.

Do you accept insurance?

Kauaʻi Drip is a cash-pay practice and does not bill insurance directly.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Appointments are required and can be booked online. Some services require a consultation before treatment.

Where are you located?

The clinic is on the second floor of the East Kauaʻi Professional Building at 4-1579 Kūhiō Highway, Suites 205 and 207, Kapaʻa. Check in at Suite 207. Parking is free, and the north-end ramp provides wheelchair-accessible access.

Health & hormone optimization

What happens during a health-optimization consultation?

Your provider reviews your symptoms, medical history, medications, lifestyle, goals and comprehensive laboratory results to develop individualized recommendations.

Do I need labs before starting hormone therapy?

Yes. Comprehensive laboratory testing is an important part of the approach. Your provider determines which labs are appropriate based on your history, symptoms and goals.

Do you treat based only on lab numbers?

No. Laboratory values are considered alongside symptoms, history, medications, lifestyle and goals.

How often will I need labs?

Timing varies by treatment and individual needs. Follow-up testing may be required periodically to monitor response, safety and dosing.

Medical weight management

Do you offer GLP-1 medications?

When medically appropriate, programs may include GLP-1–based medications with individualized nutrition, lifestyle, body-composition and metabolic-health support.

Do I need a consultation before starting?

Yes. The clinic reviews your history, medications, previous efforts, goals and appropriate laboratory testing before recommending treatment.

Is medication included in the consultation price?

No. The consultation covers the initial medical evaluation. Medication, laboratory testing and ongoing care may have separate costs.

Do you monitor more than weight?

Yes. The focus includes body composition and metabolic health. InBody 580 testing can help monitor skeletal muscle, body fat, visceral fat and other measurements.

IV therapy & wellness injections

Do I need a consultation before receiving an IV?

Every patient is medically screened. Additional evaluation or clearance may be required depending on health history and the requested treatment.

How long does an IV take?

Many traditional IV treatments take approximately 45–60 minutes. NAD+ infusions may take considerably longer depending on dose and individual tolerance.

Which IV should I choose?

It depends on your goals. The team can help you select an appropriate option after medical screening.

Can an IV be customized?

Certain treatments may be customized or supplemented with available add-ons when medically appropriate.

Do you offer NAD+?

Yes. Kauaʻi Drip offers NAD+ IV therapy and NAD+ injections.

Can you come to my hotel, rental or home?

Mobile IV visits are available island-wide, subject to screening, scheduling and a location-based mobile fee.

Peptide therapy

What are peptides?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules. Different peptides may influence different physiological processes.

Does peptide therapy require a consultation?

Yes. An individualized medical evaluation is required to determine whether treatment may be appropriate.

Are peptides appropriate for everyone?

No. Medical history, medications, health conditions, goals and potential risks must be considered.

Where do prescribed compounded medications come from?

When prescribed, compounded medications are sourced through licensed compounding pharmacies. Kauaʻi Drip does not use grey-market peptides.

Skin health & medical aesthetics

What is SkinPen microneedling?

SkinPen creates controlled microchannels that support the skin’s natural remodeling process and may improve the appearance of acne scars, fine lines, texture and overall skin quality.

What is the difference between SkinPen and SkinPen with PRP?

Standard SkinPen uses microneedling alone. The PRP option incorporates platelet-rich plasma obtained from your own blood as part of treatment.

How many microneedling treatments will I need?

It depends on your skin and goals. Some patients choose one treatment, while a series may be recommended for more significant concerns.

How do the HydraFacial options differ?

All include cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration. Higher-tier experiences add customization and treatment steps.

Do you offer medical-grade skincare?

Yes. Kauaʻi Drip offers ZO® Skin Health and can help create a personalized at-home regimen based on skin type, concerns and goals.

Body shaping, body composition & sauna

What is T-Shape 2?

T-Shape 2 is a non-invasive face and body treatment combining multiple technologies to address body contouring, skin appearance, circulation and tissue quality.

Is T-Shape 2 a weight-loss treatment?

No. It is intended for body shaping and aesthetic improvement and is not a substitute for medical weight management, nutrition or exercise.

What is an InBody scan?

The InBody 580 provides body-composition measurements including skeletal muscle mass, body-fat percentage and distribution, visceral fat and hydration-related measurements.

What is the Sunlighten infrared sauna?

The Sunlighten mPulse uses far-, mid- and near-infrared wavelengths with programs designed around relaxation, recovery, cardiovascular support and general wellness.

How long is a sauna session?

Sessions are approximately 30–45 minutes depending on the program.

How should I prepare for the sauna?

Arrive hydrated, avoid a large meal immediately beforehand, and tell the team about pregnancy, medical conditions, medications or anything affecting safe use.

Appointments, labs & policies

How should I prepare for a lab draw?

Instructions depend on the ordered labs. You may be asked to fast, hydrate, hold certain supplements or coordinate timing with medication or hormone dosing. Follow the clinic’s specific instructions.

Can I receive prescriptions without follow-up?

Prescription treatment requires appropriate monitoring. Follow-up appointments and laboratory testing may be required.

Can visitors book services?

Yes. Many wellness, IV, aesthetic, sauna and body-composition services are available to visitors, subject to screening and availability.

Do you offer private events or corporate wellness?

Yes. Customized experiences may be available for retreats, weddings, private groups and corporate wellness programs. Contact the clinic to discuss the event, location and group size.
