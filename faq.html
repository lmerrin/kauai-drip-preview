Frequently asked questions

Helpful information before you book.

These general answers do not replace individual medical advice. Your provider will determine what is appropriate for you.

General

What is Kauaʻi Drip?

A personalized health-optimization clinic offering comprehensive lab testing, hormone and health optimization, medical weight management, IV therapy, wellness injections, peptide therapy, skin-health treatments, body contouring and longevity-focused care.

Do you accept insurance?

Kauaʻi Drip is a cash-pay practice and does not bill insurance directly.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Appointments are required. Some services require a consultation, laboratory testing or medical clearance before treatment.

Can I book while visiting Kauaʻi?

Yes. Many wellness, IV, aesthetic, sauna and body-composition services are available to visitors, subject to medical screening and availability.

Health optimization & weight management

Do I need labs before hormone therapy?

Comprehensive laboratory testing is an important part of the clinic’s approach. Your provider determines which labs are appropriate based on your history, symptoms and goals.

Do you treat based only on lab numbers?

No. Lab values are considered alongside symptoms, medical history, medications, lifestyle and goals.

Do you offer GLP-1 medications?

When medically appropriate, weight-management programs may include GLP-1–based medications with individualized lifestyle, body-composition and metabolic-health support.

Is medication included in the consultation price?

No. The consultation covers the initial medical evaluation. Medication, laboratory testing and ongoing care may have separate costs.

IV therapy, peptides & aesthetics

How long does an IV take?

Many traditional IV treatments take approximately 45–60 minutes. NAD+ infusions may require more time depending on dose and individual tolerance.

Can Kauaʻi Drip come to my hotel, rental or home?

Mobile IV visits may be available island-wide. A location-based mobile fee applies. Call the clinic or use the mobile Jane category to request details.

Does peptide therapy require a consultation?

Yes. Medical history, medications, conditions, goals and possible risks must be considered first.

What is SkinPen microneedling?

SkinPen creates controlled microchannels that support the skin’s natural remodeling process. Your provider can discuss suitability, expected course and options such as PRP.

What is an InBody scan?

The InBody 580 provides body-composition measurements such as skeletal muscle mass, body fat and visceral fat.

How long is an infrared sauna session?

Sessions are approximately 30–45 minutes depending on the program selected. Arrive hydrated and disclose health conditions, pregnancy and medications to the care team.
