Peptide therapy

Peptide therapy requires an individualized medical evaluation. Health history, medications, goals and potential risks are considered before determining whether treatment may be appropriate.

What are peptides?

Peptides are small chains of amino acids that act as signals within the body. Different peptides are considered for different goals, which may relate to metabolism, recovery, body composition, sleep or healthy aging. They are not appropriate for everyone.

A consultation is required before treatment. When compounded medication is prescribed, Ashley’s intake states that it is sourced through licensed compounding pharmacies.

Skin health & aesthetics

SkinPen creates controlled microchannels that support the skin’s natural remodeling process. HydraFacial experiences combine cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration, with additional customization at higher service levels.

Compare the treatment options

SkinPen microneedling creates controlled microchannels and may be considered for concerns such as uneven texture, fine lines or the appearance of acne scars. PRP uses platelet-rich plasma obtained from your own blood as part of the treatment.

HydraFacial combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration. Deluxe and Platinum experiences include additional customization and treatment steps. Ashley can help recommend an option based on your skin and goals.

InBody body composition

The InBody 580 is a non-invasive assessment that measures more than body weight, including skeletal muscle mass, body-fat percentage and distribution, visceral-fat estimates and body-water distribution.

Why choose an InBody scan?

The scan can establish a baseline and help track changes during a wellness or weight-management program. Results should be interpreted in context and do not replace a medical evaluation.
